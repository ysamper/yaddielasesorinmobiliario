## ICP

### Primary (70%): Active Investor

**Who:** 35-55 years old. Capital available (no full financing needed). Based in mainland Spain (Madrid, Barcelona, Valencia) or Canarias. High financial literacy.

**Main pain:** Wants to invest in Tenerife real estate including NPL assets but does not understand the process, does not know which assets to trust, and has no local advisor who speaks the language of institutional transactions.

**What they've tried:** Generic real estate portals (Idealista, Fotocasa). Generalist advisors who don't know NPL. Going directly to funds without intermediary support.

**Exact customer language:**
- "No sé cómo funciona el proceso de un NPL"
- "¿Cómo sé si el activo vale realmente lo que piden?"
- "¿Quién me puede guiar sin que me cueste un error caro?"
- "Quiero diversificar fuera de la península"

### Secondary (30%): Seller Open to Exclusive Listing

**Who:** Property owner in Tenerife. Wants to sell well, fast, without complications. May have documentation complications: inheritance, horizontal division, registry delays.

**Main pain:** Doesn't trust the exclusive listing model. Believes listing with multiple agencies protects them. Doesn't know if the advisor will actually work.

**What they've tried:** Multiple agencies simultaneously. Self-listing on portals. Generalist advisors with no commitment.

**Exact customer language:**
- "No quiero atarme a un solo asesor"
- "¿Y si no se vende?"
- "¿Por qué el dinero de las arras no va a mi cuenta?"

- Short sentences. Maximum 20 words.
- Active voice always. No passive.
- No em dashes (—). Commas and periods only.
